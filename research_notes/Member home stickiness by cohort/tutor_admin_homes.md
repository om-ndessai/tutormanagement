# Signed-in homes for tutors and tutoring-business admins: what comes first, the daily action, and what brings them back

Researched 2026-11-03. Every source carries its date where it has one. Some findings come from search-result snippets of pages that were not fetched in full; those are marked "(snippet)". Pages fetched in full: TutorCruncher's tutor-dashboard post (2022), Teachworks' dashboard article (2026-08-12), Preply's Super Tutor metrics article (2026-09-16), and NN/g's dashboards article (2017).

## 1. Default landing screen and primary action: tutoring management tools and teacher tools

### Takeaway
Business-management tools split into two shapes. Business-run tools (Teachworks, TutorCruncher, Oases) open on a **metrics dashboard**. Studio and solo-teacher tools (TutorBird, My Music Staff) open on **today's agenda**, and its daily action is marking attendance from that agenda. Classroom tools (Google Classroom, Canvas) put a **queue of work to grade or review** next to the courses. Marketplaces (Wyzant, Preply) lead with **responsiveness and quality metrics** tied to the tutor's search ranking or badge. A tutor's home is mostly "my lessons plus what I owe". An owner's home is "the business's numbers plus what is overdue".

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Every tool's "unfinished work" list has an Aimtrue equivalent, and these are the strongest daily pull:
  - Teachworks' **Uncompleted Lessons** and TutorCruncher's **Actions Required** ("outstanding lessons or reports") are Aimtrue's **unrecorded lesson** (the Phase 43 "Now" card already does this in the app).
  - Google Classroom's **To review** and Canvas's **To grade** are Aimtrue's **Assessments to review** and Quest answers to review.
- Studio tools make **today's agenda** the home and let the teacher act on it in place (attendance, launching video, cancelling). The one reviewed complaint is about not being able to act from it (TutorBird). This favours an agenda where each row has its next action: Start timer, Record, Open, Cancel.
- Splitting metrics by owner versus tutor:
  - **Owner**: overdue invoices, monthly stats (active students, lessons, hours, invoiced vs. paid), profit or margin snapshot, new inquiries and requests, flagged lessons.
  - **Tutor**: own hours, own pay (last 30 days), own student count, upcoming lessons, actions required.
  - TutorCruncher's tutor dashboard shows the tutor's own money ("amount paid") at the top. Aimtrue's rule puts no money on the Tutoring tab, so for Aimtrue that metric belongs on the Finance tab, not the home.
- Two products let the landing page be chosen: Teachworks (Calendar or Dashboard, set by admins) and Khan Academy (where teachers resent Khanmigo forcing it). A forced home that does not match the user's job causes visible frustration.

### Gaps
- No current primary source on the default home of **Wise** (wiseapp.live), **Pencil Spaces**' own dashboard, the **Varsity Tutors tutor app** (only 2016 student-app launch coverage turned up), **Superprof**'s tutor dashboard, **Seesaw** teacher, or **IXL** teacher analytics. Not enough search budget remained to close these.
- No current official Google help page confirms the 2026 Classroom teacher home layout. Wayground's teacher home layout was not found.
- TutorCruncher's admin dashboard article returned 404, so the contents of the admin home are unverified.

## 2. Patterns: Today/next-lesson, "needs attention" inboxes, quick-create, KPIs, and whether each belongs to the tutor or the owner

### Takeaway
Four recurring blocks: (a) **today's agenda / upcoming lessons**, with in-place actions; (b) **a needs-attention list** (uncompleted lessons, outstanding reports, overdue invoices, flagged lessons, overdue tasks, work to grade); (c) **a short KPI strip** over a recent window (30 days for the tutor, this month for the owner); (d) **new demand** (inquiries, booking or lesson requests, jobs available). The agenda and needs-attention blocks are shared, scoped to "mine" for a tutor and to everyone for the owner. Money and growth KPIs are the owner's.

### Cited Findings
- **Today agenda**: TutorBird's Home Agenda (date stepping, "Lessons Left" this week, attendance, launch whiteboard) and My Music Staff's Home Agenda (mark Present, cancel, practice time, overdue library items). (snippet) — [TutorBird](https://www.tutorbird.com/?p=4620); [MMS](https://mymusicstaff.com/?p=5729)
- **Upcoming lessons**: Teachworks' "Upcoming Lessons" widget (a teacher's next 31 days) and TutorCruncher's "Upcoming lessons" tab, with video links reachable from the dashboard. — [Teachworks](https://intercom.help/teachworks-e2d272c6e669/en/articles/14124151-the-teachworks-dashboard); [TutorCruncher (2022)](https://tutorcruncher.com/blog/introducing-the-new-dashboard-for-tutors)
- **Needs attention, tutor side**:
  - Teachworks "Uncompleted Lessons" and "Overdue Tasks". — [Teachworks](https://intercom.help/teachworks-e2d272c6e669/en/articles/14124151-the-teachworks-dashboard)
  - TutorCruncher "Actions Required: outstanding lessons or reports". — [TutorCruncher](https://tutorcruncher.com/blog/introducing-the-new-dashboard-for-tutors)
  - Google Classroom "To review" and Canvas To Do "needs to be graded". — [T4L](https://t4l.schools.nsw.gov.au/resources/professional-learning-resources/google-resources/google-classroom0/transcript-googleclassroom-gettingstarted.html); [Canvas](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-12905)
- **Needs attention, owner side**: Teachworks "Overdue Invoices" (direct links), "Flagged Lessons", "Requested Lessons", "New Student Enrollments", "Booked Lessons". — [Teachworks](https://intercom.help/teachworks-e2d272c6e669/en/articles/14124151-the-teachworks-dashboard)
- **Outside education, the same mix of KPIs and attention items**: Stripe's Home "provides analytics and charts about your business performance. It also surfaces important notifications, like unresolved disputes or identity verifications". The overview widgets are user-editable, and the mobile Home offers 4 metric widgets (e.g. daily gross volume, new payments, new customers). — [Stripe Docs: Web Dashboard basics](https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/basics); [Stripe Docs: mobile app](https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/mobile)
- **Inbox/triage pattern (Linear)**:
  - The Inbox is the in-app notification center. People are auto-subscribed to issues they create, are assigned or are mentioned in. — [Linear Docs: Inbox](https://linear.app/docs/inbox)
  - Since 2026-09 the inbox has a **Priority** tab and an **Other** tab, with Priority chosen by default and customizable. — [Linear changelog: Priority inbox (2026-09-03)](https://linear.app/changelog/2026-09-03-priority-inbox)
  - Triage is a separate per-team inbox for issues from outside the team, kept out of normal views, with one-key accept, duplicate or decline. — [Linear Docs: Triage](https://linear.app/docs/triage)
  - Email digests are delayed by urgency and sent only if the inbox notification is still unread. — [Linear Docs: Notifications](https://linear.app/docs/notifications)
- **KPIs**:
  - Tutor: TutorCruncher shows hours taught, amount paid and students (30 days), with graphs on an Overview tab. — [TutorCruncher](https://tutorcruncher.com/blog/introducing-the-new-dashboard-for-tutors)
  - Owner: Teachworks "Monthly Statistics" (active students, teachers, lessons, hours, invoice totals, payments), "Lesson Status" (hours for 3 weeks back and 3 weeks forward), and "Lesson Profit Snapshot" (revenue, wages, gross profit). — [Teachworks](https://intercom.help/teachworks-e2d272c6e669/en/articles/14124151-the-teachworks-dashboard)
- **Quick-create**: no source in this pass documented a quick-create button on these tutoring homes. Teachworks lets add-ons contribute widgets. — [Teachworks blog (2020)](https://blog.teachworks.com/2020/09/6-teachworks-dashboard-widgets-your-business-needs/)
- **Off-app return trigger**: My Music Staff's opt-in daily agenda email at a time the teacher chooses. (snippet) — [MMS](https://www.mymusicstaff.com/?p=64549)

### Inferences
- A per-role home for Aimtrue could look like this:
  - **Tutor**: the Now/next lesson, then today's agenda with in-row actions, then "Needs you" (unrecorded lessons, Assessments to review, Quest answers to review, reflections to read), then a small no-money KPI strip (hours or lessons this week, students).
  - **Owner/admin**: "Needs you" first (unrecorded lessons across tutors, overdue family balances, tutor top-ups due, join requests and invitations, flagged items), then this month's numbers on the Finance tab, then today across all tutors.
- Linear's Priority/Other split and Stripe's "analytics plus attention items" both suggest the attention list should be ranked and short, with the rest behind "View all", as Google Classroom does.
- The email digest that goes out only while an item is unread (Linear), and the opt-in daily agenda email (MMS), are documented ways to bring people back off-platform without nagging.

### Gaps
- No usage data (click-through, time to first action) was found for any of these widgets. The split between owner and tutor KPIs above comes from how the vendors word their widgets, not from published research.

## 3. Marketplace nudges: Preply, Wyzant, Varsity Tutors, Superprof

### Takeaway
Marketplaces keep tutors active with **metrics they can see that gate ranking or a badge**. The core metrics are speed of first reply, trial conversion, attendance, cancellations and reschedules, and use of the platform's own classroom. Each runs on a rolling window (Preply 90 days; Wyzant 60 days, recalculated hourly). The only published effect numbers found are vendor claims: Preply says a pre-trial message raises conversion by 10%, and Wyzant says its most successful tutors reply in under 6 hours.

### Cited Findings
- Preply's Super Tutor criteria (article dated 2026-09-16), tracked on the tutor's **Insights page**:
  1. **Pre-trial engagement**: message ≥80% of students within 24 h of a new message or a trial booking. Before 2026-08-01 this was 90% of first messages within 24 h.
  2. **Trial-to-subscription conversion**: confirmed or auto-confirmed trials only; waived below 3 trials in 90 days.
  3. **Cancellation and reschedule rates**: tracked separately for trial and regular lessons.
  4. **Attendance**: "If you are marked as absent from a lesson, it will **immediately** impact your Super Tutor status". Attendance counts only after clicking "Enter the classroom".
  5. **Preply Classroom usage** over 90 days, plus, from 2026-08-01, ≥80% of trial lessons held in the Classroom.
  6. **Post-trial engagement**, added 2026-08-01: message ≥80% of students within 24 h after a trial. A "next steps" prompt appears about 2 h after the trial.

  Source: [Preply help (2026-09-16)](http://help.preply.com/en/articles/14880665-how-super-tutor-metrics-and-criteria-work)
- Preply's own effect claim: "Tutors who send a message before a trial increase conversions by 10%." — [Preply help](http://help.preply.com/en/articles/14880665-how-super-tutor-metrics-and-criteria-work)
- A 2024 tutor newsletter reported a conversion-rate threshold rising to 60% that January. This is anecdotal and possibly outdated. (snippet) — [Koalabites newsletter (Oct 2024)](https://koalabites-newsletter-624e17.beehiiv.com/p/preply-update-oct-2024)
- Wyzant:
  - Response rate and response time measure "how quickly you respond to the first messages from potential new students", recalculated hourly over 60 days. Students see only the time, never the rate.
  - "Your response rate affects your ranking in student search results." Replying "No" does not hurt it, and only inquiries within the tutor's contact hours count toward the time.

  Source: [Wyzant support: response rate and response time](https://support.wyzant.com/?p=392)
- "The most successful tutors listed on Wyzant respond to students in fewer than 6 hours." (snippet) — [Wyzant support: appear higher in search](https://support.wyzant.com/?p=487)
- An older press piece (NBC News, undated in the snippet) says reviews and use of Wyzant's payments and scheduling also affect ranking. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna56041429)

### Inferences
- Metrics that change ranking or status ("immediately") are a stick as well as a carrot. Aimtrue has no marketplace ranking, so its nearest equivalents are the tutor's own operational hygiene: lessons recorded within a day, assessments reviewed within N days, reflections read. These can be shown as personal streaks or counts in the spirit of Phase 64's "habit layer is the reader's own", without ranking tutors against each other.
- Preply's ~2 h "next steps" prompt after a trial is a timed nudge tied to an event. The Aimtrue equivalent is the "lesson just finished, record it now" card.

### Gaps
- The benefits of Preply Super Tutor status are not stated in the metrics article. Varsity Tutors' tutor app and Superprof's tutor dashboard turned up nothing current. No independent (non-vendor) effect sizes for any marketplace nudge were found.

## 4. Evidence on dashboard design for operational users: role-based, action-oriented versus analytics, and the owner who also teaches

### Takeaway
NN/g separates **operational** dashboards (critical information fast, for time-sensitive work) from **analytical** ones (prompting deeper investigation). Both should be single-screen and glanceable. Today Aimtrue's tutor/admin home is mostly analytical (stat cards, a spotlight, recent activity), while the daily jobs of a tutor (teach, record, review) and an owner (chase what is overdue) are operational. Tools handle role-based homes with per-role widget permissions and a choice of landing page. No source documents a dedicated mode for an owner who also teaches; the common answer is "mine" widgets beside business widgets on one dashboard.

### Cited Findings
- "Operational dashboards aim to impart critical information quickly to users as they are engaged in time-sensitive tasks." Analytical dashboards "don't have the same level of time sensitivity" and help users decide when deeper analysis is needed. A dashboard's goal "is not to facilitate exploration", and analytical ones still need "an at-a-glance, single-screen view". — [NN/g, Page Laubheimer, "Dashboards: Making Charts and Graphs Easier to Understand" (2017-06-18; older)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dashboards-preattentive/)
- NN/g on charts: prefer length and 2D position (bar, line, scatter). Avoid pies, donuts, gauges and treemaps on "simple, actionable dashboards". Use color for category, not magnitude. — [same](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dashboards-preattentive/)
- Role-based homes in tutoring software:
  - Teachworks admins set which widgets staff and teachers see "based on profile widget permissions", and can make the landing page Calendar or Dashboard. (snippet) — [Teachworks help](https://intercom.help/teachworks-e2d272c6e669/en/articles/11472025)
  - TutorCruncher has a separate tutor dashboard showing that tutor's own 30-day hours, pay and students. — [TutorCruncher (2022)](https://tutorcruncher.com/blog/introducing-the-new-dashboard-for-tutors)
- The owner who also teaches:
  - Teachworks mixes teacher-scoped widgets ("a Teacher's upcoming/uncompleted lessons") with business widgets (Monthly Statistics, Overdue Invoices, Profit Snapshot) on one configurable dashboard. — [Teachworks](https://intercom.help/teachworks-e2d272c6e669/en/articles/14124151-the-teachworks-dashboard)
  - TutorBird's multi-teacher calendar day view shows only the selected teachers, so an owner can narrow it to themselves. (snippet) — [TutorBird](https://www.tutorbird.com/?p=14251)
- Combined action and analytics home (Stripe): charts plus "important notifications, like unresolved disputes". — [Stripe Docs](https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/basics)
- A forced home that does not fit the job gets pushback: Khan Academy teachers object that Khanmigo's Tools page replaced their teacher dashboard as the homepage. (snippet) — [Khan Academy community](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/da/community/posts/31217984889613/comments/31576181690637)

### Inferences
- For Aimtrue, the evidence supports **an operational home first, with analytics one tap away**. This matches the Phase 43 app direction, which moved Analytics to More → Dashboard and leads with a Now card.
- For an admin who tutors, the documented pattern (Teachworks) is "my teaching" blocks beside "the business" blocks, with the business's needs-attention items first. A second option is a "Me / Everyone" scope toggle like TutorBird's teacher filter. Aimtrue's rule that "a role's dashboard shows that role's data only" means both blocks would need clear labels.
- Making the landing page configurable (Teachworks) is a cheap way to cover owners who live in the calendar.

### Gaps
- No published Intercom or NN/g study was found that compares retention under an action-oriented home against an analytics home. NN/g's dashboard article dates from 2017. No research was found on the dual-role owner-tutor beyond product configuration. No product published retention or DAU effects from a home redesign.
