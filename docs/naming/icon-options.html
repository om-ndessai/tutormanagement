Eight other icons for Aimtrue

You were right about the arrow and ring. Below are eight replacements, each drawn the way you will really meet it: on the home screen, at small sizes, in other colours, and as the plain mark the app shows on an organization's own colour.

What was wrong with the current one. An arrow going into a circle is the standard symbol for “sign in”, and it is close to the symbol for “male”. The lines are thin and the faded ring disappears at small sizes. The new ones are bolder, each has one idea, and each adds a warm accent for the goal.

Option 1

Tick A

A for aim, ticked true.

Inside the app, on an organization's colour

A capital A whose crossbar is a tick. It reads as a letter first, so it is unmistakably yours, and the tick says “true” and “done”. An A is also the top mark in school.

Weakness. The tick has to stay bold enough to read at the smallest size.

How to choose

  • Look at the smallest tile first. That is the size in Settings and in notifications. If you cannot tell what it is there, the large version does not matter.
  • The amber is new. Until now the icon was one colour. Every option works without it, as the bottom row of each card shows, because an organization's default mark has to sit on its own colour.
