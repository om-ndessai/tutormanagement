# The question bank, round 6: Math Kangaroo

Requested by the owner on 2026-10-27: "create question bank for math kangaroo competitions. Review the Math
Kangaroo past 10 years question papers and start working on each level question banks." The levels are built
one at a time: each level is committed and pushed, then reviewed by the owner (with the credit it used)
before the next one starts.

## The papers reviewed

The international Kangaroo papers in English, 2016-2025, from matematica.pt (the owner's source):
- one paper a year for each of the contest's six levels, and the answer keys;
- the levels are Felix (grades 1-2), Ecolier (3-4), Benjamin (5-6), Kadett (7-8), Junior (9-10) and
  Student (11-12);
- Math Kangaroo USA uses the same six pairs of grades.

The grades 1-2 papers are the Austrian edition (15 questions) in most years and the Brazilian one (24) in
2020-2021. Both come in thirds worth 3, 4 and 5 points, with five options each.

The papers are copyrighted and are not in the repository. They were read to learn what the contest asks;
no question, figure or text of theirs is reproduced. Every question in the bank is original, written from
our own topic summaries (below), as with Beast Academy (`docs/question-bank-beast-academy.md`).

**What the grades 1-2 papers ask** (about 168 questions over ten years, sorted by hand, roughly):

| Kind | Share | Examples of the idea (in our words) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pictures and space | about 45% | views of a stack of cubes, mirror images, which jigsaw piece fits, folding and punching paper, paths in a maze |
| Number and calculation | about 20% | missing numbers, symbols standing for numbers, equal sums around a shape |
| Logic and order | about 15% | taller-than chains, places in a queue or a race, who sleeps in which basket |
| Story problems | about 15% | legs and humps, lit windows, points per hit, frogs singing on nine nights |
| Counting ways | about 5% | two-digit numbers from given digits, dancing pairs, how many to take to be sure |

The bank is text only: the Assessment's PDF and Quest show no pictures. So about half of what the youngest
papers ask, the picture puzzles, cannot be carried over as it is. Where the idea survives in words, it is
kept in "Shapes and Space in Words": glue between cubes in a row, the faces of a die, folding and cutting,
turns on a walk, and letters in a mirror. The other kinds carry over whole.

## How a question is written and checked

The same pipeline as the earlier rounds, run by Claude Code agents outside the Worker (no `ai_runs` rows):

1. **Writing.** A Claude Sonnet writer per two topics writes 30 a topic, 10 per difficulty, about two
   thirds choice and one third numeric. It works from the topic summary and a style brief distilled from
   the review: short stories, one small idea each, every arrangement written out in words, every
   assumption stated ("both included", "each a different number").
2. **Code checks.** `lintQuestion` (form, four distinct options with exactly one the key, `compute`
   evaluated exactly, `$` balance, near-copies), plus no reference to a picture. Money is written in words,
   never with `$`, which is the maths delimiter.
3. **The blind solve.** A Claude Opus solver works each question with the options shuffled and no key.
   `checkAnswer` must match its answer, and it judges single answer, topic, grade and errors.
4. Disagreements are rewritten and solved again. Only `checked` questions are kept. A platform admin
   approves them in the console, or a tutor does through Quest.

The contest has five options; the bank has four, as every question in it does.

## Code

- `db/catalog/mk.json`: the curriculum and, for now, its `MK12` level.
- `QUESTION_BANK_LEVEL_IDS` adds `MK12`, after the AoPS books, labelled "Kangaroo 1-2".
- `assessmentLevelCurriculum` names `mk` for it. Like Beast Academy and NC 2017, the level appears in the
  Assessment menu, Quest and Quest Games only where the organization offers Math Kangaroo on its Curricula
  card.
- `QUEST_LEVEL_FAMILIES` gains a Math Kangaroo family, which grows level by level.
- `db/questions/MK12.json` holds the questions; `generate-questions.mjs` accepts the level.
- No schema change.

## Release

Not released. The work is on its branch; production and the demo are untouched. When the owner releases it,

1. The local suite and `npm run smoke` green.
2. Back up production.
3. Run `db/catalog.sql` there (upserts; it adds the `mk` rows).
4. Load the new questions as `checked` with `generate-questions.mjs --production` (INSERTs only,
   `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`).
5. Deploy both Workers.

## Results

### Grades 1-2 (2026-10-27)

240 questions, all `checked`, none approved: 30 a topic, 10 per difficulty, 160 choice and 80 numeric. The bank
goes from 40,738 to 40,978.

- **Written:** 240 by four Sonnet writers (two topics each), clean on the code checks first time round,
  except a few options that carried units ("21 cm"), which the writers moved into the stem.
- **The blind solve:** four Opus solvers agreed with every key (240 of 240). They marked 4 down for wording,
  each with the right answer:
  - "with no number skipped" while counting in 2s;
  - "up to 20 seconds", which could leave out the 20th;
  - "turns right round" in a question about left and right;
  - a corner cut that could run from a neighbouring corner.
  All four were reworded and solved blind again, and all passed.
- **Giveaways:** the key is strictly the longest option in 2 of the 160 choice questions and strictly the
  shortest in none. Number options are in increasing order, as in the contest.
- **Notes per topic** are in `db/questions/MK12.json`. The briefs and scripts are in
  `docs/question-bank-kangaroo/`, and `ids-MK12.json` keeps each draft's id stable across repairs.

**What it cost** (the subagents' own tokens, besides the coordinating session):
- writing: about 476,000 Sonnet tokens;
- the blind solve and the re-solve: about 452,000 Opus tokens.

Next: grades 3-4 (Ecolier), once the owner has reviewed this level.

### Grades 3-4 (2026-10-27)

240 questions, all `checked`: 30 a topic, 10 per difficulty, 160 choice and 80 numeric.

- **Blind solve:** the solvers agreed with every key (240 of 240). They marked 4 down for wording:
  - "every 4th bulb is also blue", which can be read as also red;
  - when a lift's turn at the bottom floor counts as a stop;
  - twice, "a border goes all the way round", which could be the mat's own outer ring.
  All four were reworded and passed a second blind solve.
- **Giveaways:** the key is strictly the longest option in 4 of the 160 choice questions and strictly the
  shortest in 7.
- **Originality:** one stem shares a 10-word run with a paper: the number pattern "1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 4, ...",
  written out, which is not a problem's wording.
- **Cost:**
  - review of the papers: about 125,000 Sonnet tokens;
  - writing: about 520,000 Sonnet;
  - blind solve and re-solve: about 440,000 Opus. One solver's count was lost in a container restart and
    is estimated.
